## Part A - Survey Design: 

The number of your chosen topic: 1

Describe the purpose of your survey:

The purpose of the survey is to identify key patterns surrounding the differentially high turnover rate among early level positions relative to previous months and relative to higher level positions. The standardized survey will cover questions on job progression, company culture, and an exit interview. 

Describe your target population, sampling frame, sampling units, and observational units:

The target population is all current and recently separated (e.g. terminated) employees of the tech firm. The survey should not simply focus on current employees as this introduces selection bias (e.g. these employees chose to stay in addition to not being fired). Equally focusing on employees who leave the firm (voluntarily or not) would also introduce selection bias. Finally, the survey should not focus only on the lower-level positions. Including higher positions allows to capture progression through the ranks and provides another comparison group.

The sampling frame is going to be company directory of all current employees and records of recently departed employees.

The sampling units are the employees who receive the invitation to complete the survey. The survey will be distributed to all current and recent employees.

The observations units are going to be the employees who chose to answer the survey. Even if the survey is mandatory, it is likely that not everyone will answer it. Non-response bias will be particularly salient for the former employees.

## Part B - Survey Evaluation:

Identify and describe survey features:

The sample type is cross-sectional sampling based on probabilities. Specifically it uses stratified (by province and population group), two-stage probability sampling. The first-stage is conducted at the household level. The second is at the individual level. 

The sample size is 16 149 respondents. The target sample size was 20 000.

The target population is persons aged 15 or older, excluding residents of the Territories, First-Nations Reserves and individuals living in institutions (eg. long term care homes or prisons).

The sampling frame is based on lists of telephone numbers in use and a list of all dwellings within the ten provinces (Address Registrar).

The survey mode uses a mixed approach with telephone and online options.

The collection period was September to December 2018.

The response rate for was 41.9%. 

The observations in the survey are weighted to represent the target population, including those not covered by the survey frame. 

The data undergo some cleaning and validity checks, for example, removing duplicate record, correcting missing or incorrect information.  They are also anonymized for public release.

The survey is subject to standard sources of error such as sampling error (inherent to probability sampling and relatively small sample size), nonresponse error, coverage error, and measurement error (e.g. different understanding of the questions or recall bias).

A limitation of this survey is that it may not be compared directly to previous editions from the inclusion of the internet responses. In addition, the iterations of this survey do not form a panel, i.e. do not survey the same individuals over time. Instead, it is a repeated cross-section.  

Link to documentation and any additional sources used: The data guide available for download at: https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/n1/pub/45-25-0001/cat5/c33_2018.zip
